# ChecklistApp

ChecklistApp is a private, non-commercial app for a touch display in a kitchen. It shows each user's daily routine, chores, appointments and tasks, with points and levels that make getting things done more fun.

Each user can connect their own Google account. The app then shows their appointments from Google Calendar (read only) and their tasks from Google Tasks. Ticking a task on the display marks it as done in Google Tasks too.

The app isn't sold or offered for download. It is used only by a few people on one private display.
